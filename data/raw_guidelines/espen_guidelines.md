# ESPEN Guidelines on Clinical Nutrition and Micronutrients

## ESPEN_2023_P21: Macronutrient Distribution in Metabolic Disorders
In patients with metabolic disorders, carbohydrate quality is paramount. A distribution of 40% to 45% of total energy from complex, low-glycemic-index carbohydrates combined with 15% to 20% protein and 30% to 35% healthy fats promotes metabolic flexibility, lipid normalization, and long-term glycemic stability.

## ESPEN_2023_P22: Renal Compromise and Protein Restriction
In patients exhibiting compromised renal function with an estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (eGFR) < 30 mL/min/1.73m2 (Stage 4 to 5 CKD not on maintenance dialysis):
- High-protein diets (> 1.0 g/kg body weight/day) accelerate intraglomerular hypertension and disease progression.
- Dietary protein should be strictly restricted to 0.6 to 0.8 g/kg body weight/day (or <= 10-12% of total daily calories). High protein supplements and ketogenic diets are contraindicated.

## ESPEN_2023_P23: Micronutrient Synergy and Anti-Inflammatory Balance
Adequate intake of antioxidant vitamins (vitamins C and E) and trace minerals (zinc, selenium, iron) reduces systemic oxidative stress in chronic inflammatory states. Micronutrient supplementation should be guided by documented biochemical deficiencies rather than supra-therapeutic empiric doses.
